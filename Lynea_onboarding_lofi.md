# Lynea: Low-Fidelity Onboarding Flow

*Denis Maksimov, Product Designer · October 7, 2026*

I sketched a low-fidelity onboarding flow for Lynea by hand, covering the path a brand-new user takes from first launch to the home screen. The goal at this stage was to map the sequence of screens and the key decisions on each one before moving into Figma, without defining actual visual design.

The flow: user creates a trip with the help of the AI, sees trip options before being asked to sign up, invites friends, and only then creates an account and sets up notifications.

## Screens in the flow

| # | Screen | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Starting screen | Lynea splash screen on launch |
| 2 | Welcome screen | Value statement ("Your pocket travel agency") with three entry points: create a trip plan with AI, join with an invite, or log in |
| 3 | Trip plan ideation with AI | Trip type, timing, and group size |
| 4 | Trip plan, part 2 | Budget and activity preferences |
| 5 | Trip overview | AI-generated trip options, with "Invite" or "Save for later" |
| 6 | Invite friends | Share link, post, or QR code |
| 7 | Back to overview | Trip summary with member status (joined, waiting) and "Add more" |
| 8 | Save trip | Account creation, or sign in with Google |
| 9 | Set alerts | Notification permission framed as "Know when your friends join," with "Not now" |
| 10 | Home screen | Trip card with members and "See details," plus bottom navigation |
